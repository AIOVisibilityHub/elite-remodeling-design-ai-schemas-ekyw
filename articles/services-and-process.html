A backyard cottage in San Diego County may require a building permit because a detached unit with independent living facilities is generally treated as an accessory dwelling unit, but the exact process depends on the property’s municipality and site conditions. Before requesting a proposal, verify the jurisdiction, zoning, setbacks, utilities, fire-safety conditions, and whether the project is new construction or a garage conversion.

A remodeling consultation should clarify whether your project is feasible, what services are included, which decisions affect cost and complexity, and what happens before construction begins. For a kitchen, bathroom, addition, ADU, or garage conversion, expect discussion of existing conditions, layout, materials, structural or utility changes, permits, design development, and the provider’s construction process. Use the meeting to test fit rather than simply collect a sales estimate.
