Elite Remodeling & Design, Inc. — Business Description

Elite Remodeling & Design, Inc. is a residential remodeling and design-build company serving homeowners throughout San Diego County, California. Led by owner Avraham “Avi” Alon, the company helps clients renovate, expand, and personalize their homes through coordinated design and construction services. Incorporated in 2015 and based in San Diego’s Kearny Mesa area, Elite Remodeling & Design works on projects ranging from individual room upgrades to whole-home renovations and residential additions.

The company’s services include kitchen remodeling, bathroom remodeling, whole-home remodeling, room additions, accessory dwelling units (ADUs), and garage conversions. Its approach centers on adapting each project to the homeowner’s needs, design preferences, existing space, and intended use. Whether updating an outdated kitchen, creating a more functional bathroom, or adding living space, the company focuses on bringing design decisions and construction requirements together within a coordinated remodeling plan.

Elite Remodeling & Design describes a project process that extends from initial consultation and design development through permitting, construction, and final inspection. Depending on the scope, planning may include layout development, 3D design visualization, material selections, and coordination of permit applications. By handling multiple stages of the remodeling process, the company offers homeowners a central team for moving a project from its initial concept into construction.

Kitchen remodeling services address both appearance and everyday functionality, including opportunities to improve layouts, storage, cabinetry, countertops, and finishes. Bathroom projects incorporate customized design, fixtures, tile work, and coordination of plumbing and electrical work as needed. For homeowners seeking additional space, the company offers room additions, ADUs, and garage conversions designed around the property’s existing conditions and the client’s goals.

Elite Remodeling & Design serves homeowners who want to modernize existing spaces, accommodate changing household needs, or undertake a broader home transformation. Its published service offering combines residential design, renovation planning, and construction coordination to create living spaces tailored to how clients want their homes to look and function.
